Welcome to Lazeez Foods

Where Traditions
Are Honoured

At Lazeez Foods, every moment is made memorable. We are a strictly Halaal family restaurant and take-away in the heart of Malvern, Durban — dedicated to authentic Indian cuisine crafted with love, care, and pride.

From open fire tikka grills to slow-cooked biryanis and traditional curries, every dish is prepared fresh with a signature blend of hand-selected spices that honour timeless culinary heritage.
